What Is Invisalign®?

Invisalign® is a clear aligner system that gradually moves teeth using a series of custom-made removable trays. Each aligner is designed to apply gentle pressure to specific teeth, helping improve alignment without brackets or wires.

At Blue Whale Dental, Invisalign® treatment is planned digitally so you can better understand your smile goals, treatment steps, and expected progress before beginning care.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Invisalign®?

Invisalign® is an excellent solution for many teens and adults looking to straighten their teeth discreetly. The best candidates are committed to wearing their aligners consistently and maintaining good oral health throughout treatment.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions about Invisalign clear aligners in Midtown NYC? Find quick answers to the questions we receive most frequently.

Is Invisalign® painful?
Most patients experience a mild, temporary feeling of pressure during the first few days of starting a new aligner tray set. This is a positive sign that the aligners are gently shifting your teeth into their proper positions. The smooth SmartTrack® plastic is significantly more comfortable than traditional metal brackets and wires.
How long does Invisalign® treatment take?
The duration of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case. On average, a standard adult treatment takes between 12 to 18 months, though noticeable results can begin appearing in just a few weeks. Consistency in wearing your trays for 20-22 hours a day is the single most important factor.
How often do I need to wear my clear aligners?
For optimal results, aligners should be worn between 20 to 22 hours per day. They should only be removed for meals, snacks, drinking hot or sugary beverages, and daily brushing and flossing routines.
Will Invisalign® affect my speech?
Some patients may experience a very slight, temporary lisp for the first day or two of starting Invisalign treatment as their tongue adjusts to the trays. This minor effect disappears rapidly as the tongue adapts, and your normal speech patterns will quickly return.
Does dental insurance cover Invisalign®?
Many modern dental insurance policies provide partial coverage for orthodontic treatments, including Invisalign. At Blue Whale Dental, we accept a wide range of insurances and offer flexible internal payment plans to make your dental care stress-free and highly affordable.
