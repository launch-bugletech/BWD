Discreet & Removable

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Straighten your smile without metal braces. Invisalign® clear aligners are virtually invisible, fully removable, and designed for busy professionals, teens, and adults who want effective results with minimal disruption to daily life.

  • Nearly invisible, custom-fit trays
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing
  • Advanced digital treatment planning

Traditional Braces

For more complex alignment issues, traditional braces remain one of the most effective and reliable orthodontic solutions. Our team uses modern bracket systems designed for comfort, efficiency, and lasting results.

  • Effective for complex bite and alignment cases
  • Modern, lower-profile bracket systems
  • Suitable for teens and adults alike

Retainers

Side-by-Side Comparison

Invisalign® vs. Traditional Braces

Not sure which option fits your lifestyle? Here's a quick comparison to help guide your conversation with our orthodontic team.

Feature Invisalign® Clear Aligners Traditional Braces
Appearance Nearly invisible, clear plastic trays Visible metal or ceramic brackets and wires
Removability Fully removable for eating, brushing & flossing Fixed in place for the full treatment duration
Best For Mild to moderate alignment issues, busy professionals Complex bite and alignment cases, all ages
Maintenance Easy cleaning, no food restrictions Requires care around certain foods and extra brushing
Check-In Visits Periodic digital progress check-ins Regular in-office adjustment appointments

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

Have more questions about your treatment options? Our team is happy to discuss everything during your consultation.

Which orthodontic treatment is right for me?
The best treatment depends on your alignment needs, lifestyle, and goals. During your consultation, our Midtown Manhattan orthodontic team will evaluate your smile and recommend options such as Invisalign®, traditional braces, retainers, or pediatric orthodontic care.
How much does orthodontic treatment cost in NYC?
Costs vary depending on the treatment type and complexity of your case. We provide personalized treatment plans and discuss all costs during your consultation so you can make an informed decision.
Is orthodontic treatment painful?
Most patients experience mild discomfort or pressure after adjustments, which typically subsides within a few days. Our team focuses on gentle techniques and clear aftercare guidance to keep you comfortable throughout treatment.
How long does orthodontic treatment take?
Treatment length depends on the option chosen and the complexity of your case. Invisalign and braces treatments often range from several months to a couple of years. We'll outline a personalized timeline during your consultation.
Do you treat both adults and children?
Yes. We offer orthodontic care for patients of all ages, from early pediatric evaluations to Invisalign and braces for teens and adults.
Does dental insurance cover orthodontics?
Many dental insurance policies provide partial coverage for orthodontic treatment. At Blue Whale Dental, we accept a wide range of insurances and offer flexible internal payment plans to make care more affordable.
Why choose Blue Whale Dental for orthodontics in Midtown Manhattan?
Blue Whale Dental combines advanced orthodontic technology, personalized treatment planning, and a patient-focused experience in the heart of Midtown Manhattan. Our goal is to help patients of all ages achieve confident, healthy smiles.
